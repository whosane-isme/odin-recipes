Fried Chicken Breast

This Chicken is Fried!!

Ingredients:

  • oil
  • chicken breast
  • salt
  • pepper
  • butter
  • flour

    • Note: SERVE WITH SAUCE

Steps:

  1. Preheat pan or whatever to 350°F (175°C)
  2. Season chicken breast with salt and pepper
  3. Put chicken breast in pan and cook until golden brown
  4. Remove from pan and let cool before serving
